# Data card — Replica

## 1. Datasets

| Cohort | Role | Source | Raw patients | Analysis OS | Analysis DFS/RFS |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| TCGA-BRCA PanCancer Atlas | Reference / discovery | cBioPortal `brca_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018` | 1,084 | 868 | 763 |
| METABRIC | External validation | cBioPortal `brca_metabric` | 2,509 | 1,173 | 1,172 |
| TCGA-BRCA Firehose Legacy | Clinical supplement (ER/PR) | cBioPortal `brca_tcga` | 1,101 | — | — |

The TCGA Firehose cohort is used only to supply ER/PR by IHC to the PanCancer patients. It is not an independent cohort: all 1,084 PanCancer patients are present in Firehose. Joining by `patientId` is therefore a within-patient annotation merge, not external validation.

## 2. Why these cohorts

TCGA-BRCA and METABRIC are the two most widely used public breast cancer cohorts with published intrinsic subtype calls and mature survival follow-up. They differ in platform, era, case mix, and treatment, which makes them a useful stress test for transportability. The audit does not pool them into a single training set; TCGA is the reference/discovery cohort (it supplies the locked model and NMF modules) and METABRIC is external validation.

## 4. Harmonisation rules

Subtype labels map to LumA, LumB, Basal, Her2, Normal, and claudin-low. The primary analysis uses the four original intrinsic classes. Normal and claudin-low are analysed separately as sensitivity analyses and are never silently merged.

Stage is harmonised to I/II vs III/IV. TCGA stage strings such as `STAGE IIA` map to II; METABRIC numeric stages 1–4 map directly. Stage 0 (in-situ) and stage X are treated as unknown in the invasive cohort.

ER and PR are positive vs negative; `Indeterminate` is treated as missing. HER2 is positive vs negative and is used only in METABRIC sensitivity analyses.

Times are months. Non-positive times are excluded from that endpoint. Missing times or statuses are not imputed.

## 5. Attrition

The analysis-ready cohort is a complete-case cohort for the primary clinical covariates (age, stage, ER, PR) and the four-class subtype. The exact counts are written to `artifacts/tables/attrition.csv` on every run. The main losses are:

- TCGA: 103 patients without a PAM50 call, 36 with `Normal`, and 52/55 with missing ER/PR; the complete-case OS cohort is 868.
- METABRIC: 529 patients without a Claudin/PAM50 call, 218 claudin-low, 148 Normal, 6 NC, and 745 without a sample-level stage; the complete-case OS cohort is 1,173.

Complete-case analysis is the prespecified primary approach. Median/mode imputation plus missingness indicators is a prespecified extension, not a result reported in this release. The missingness is not assumed to be missing completely at random.

## 6. Expression data

The expression panel is a 995-gene subset of the MSigDB Hallmark union. For each of the 50 Hallmark pathways, the 20 genes that occur in the fewest Hallmark sets were selected (ties alphabetical), then mapped to Entrez IDs through the cBioPortal gene list. This selection is fixed before outcome analysis and does not use survival.

TCGA expression comes from `brca_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018_rna_seq_v2_mrna` (RSEM, log2(x+1) applied). METABRIC expression comes from `brca_metabric_mrna` (Illumina HT-12 v3 microarray, already log2). Genes are z-scored within cohort before module scoring. Cross-platform absolute values are not compared; only within-cohort relative scores are used.

The raw expression matrices are not committed to the repository because they are large and redistributed by cBioPortal. The download scripts and checksums reproduce them.

## 7. Ethics and governance

Both cohorts are public, de-identified research resources. TCGA data are generated by the TCGA Research Network and distributed through GDC/cBioPortal. METABRIC data are distributed through cBioPortal. Users must follow the source terms. Replica does not attempt re-identification and does not serve patient-level records through the public API.

The dashboard and API expose aggregate estimates only. No individual survival prediction is offered as a clinical service.

## 8. Known limitations

The subtype labels are not assay-identical. TCGA PAM50 calls and METABRIC PAM50/Claudin-low calls were produced by different pipelines. The clinical covariates are not measured identically. TCGA grade is absent from the cBioPortal export. DFS and RFS are different endpoints. Missingness is substantial and may be informative. The cohorts differ in treatment era and regimen. None of these limitations is repaired by a larger permutation count.
